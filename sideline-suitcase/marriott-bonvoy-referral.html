Why a sports family might want it

  • Lots of Marriott brands sit near convention centers and sports complexes (Courtyard, Residence Inn, Fairfield, SpringHill Suites, TownePlace).
  • Points from hotel stays and everyday spending can add up over a competition season.
  • The cards come with perks like free night awards or elite night credits, depending on which card you choose. Check the current benefits for each.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone who might carry a balance. Interest costs more than points are worth.
  • Families that mostly stay with a different hotel chain.
  • Anyone planning a mortgage or car loan soon who doesn't want a new credit inquiry.
