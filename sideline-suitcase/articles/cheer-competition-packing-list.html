The Cheer Competition Packing List I Actually Use

By Kylee · Updated October 28, 2026

Every item here earned its spot after a forgotten bow, a broken zipper, or a 6:40 a.m. check-in. Print it, screenshot it, or pin it.

Short version: pack the uniform and shoes in a carry-on that never leaves your sight, bring a full hair and makeup kit with spares, pack real food, and add a small hotel kit (power strip, steamer, laundry bag). Put everything else in the big suitcase.

1. The never-leaves-my-hand bag

  • Uniform, on a hanger, inside a garment bag
  • Cheer shoes (in a shoe bag so they stay clean)
  • Bow, plus a backup bow if your gym sells them
  • Practice wear for warm-ups
  • Athlete ID or credential and any wristbands
  • Event schedule, screenshotted (venue Wi-Fi is never good)

2. Hair and makeup kit

  • Team-required makeup (eyeshadow, lipstick, lashes and lash glue)
  • Makeup remover wipes, because the drive home is long
  • Hair ties, bobby pins, hairspray, gel, brush and comb
  • Curling iron or the gym's required tool, plus a heat mat
  • Small mirror with a stand
  • Rhinestone glue or touch-up items if your team uses them

3. Snacks and drinks

  • Refillable water bottle for each kid
  • Protein snacks that don't melt: nuts (if allowed), jerky, crackers, granola bars
  • Fruit that travels well: clementines, apples, grapes
  • A small cooler for the hotel room
  • Electrolyte packets

Check the venue's outside-food rules before you load the wagon. Some convention centers don't allow coolers.

4. Hotel room kit

  • Travel steamer for the uniform
  • Power strip or multi-port charger (there are never enough outlets near the mirror)
  • Laundry bag to keep sweaty practice wear separate
  • Night light and a white-noise app for the younger siblings
  • Disinfecting wipes for the room

5. Mom (and sibling) survival kit

  • Seat cushion and a light blanket; arenas run cold
  • Portable phone battery
  • Pain reliever, bandages, athletic tape, blister pads
  • Tablet, headphones, and a coloring book for siblings
  • Cash for parking, programs and the spirit-wear booth
  • A collapsible wagon if the walk from parking is long

What to leave at home

Extra outfits "just in case," full-size toiletries the hotel already provides, and anything valuable you'd be upset to lose in a crowded warm-up room.

Quick answers

What should a cheerleader pack for a competition?

The uniform, shoes and bow in a carry-on bag that stays with you; a full hair and makeup kit with spare bobby pins and lash glue; refillable water and non-melting snacks; practice wear; and any athlete credentials. Add a steamer and power strip for the hotel.

How early should we arrive at a cheer competition?

Follow your gym's report time, and add time for parking, security lines and walking from the garage. At large events, parking alone can take a long time on the morning of the first day.

Can you bring food into a cheer competition?

It depends on the venue. Many allow sealed snacks and empty water bottles but not coolers. Check the event's spectator information page before you go.
