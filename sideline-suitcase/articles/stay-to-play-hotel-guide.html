Stay-to-Play Hotels: What Cheer and Sports Parents Need to Know

By Kylee · Updated October 28, 2026

If you've ever been told you "have to" book through the event's housing link, this is why, and how to make it work for your family.

Short version: many big cheer, dance, volleyball and travel-ball events require teams to book through an official housing company. That's "stay-to-play." Book the official block early, read the cancellation terms, and add your hotel loyalty number to the reservation so the stay still counts toward points and status.

What does stay-to-play mean?

Event producers sign contracts with hotels near the venue. In exchange, they ask teams to book rooms inside that hotel block, usually through a housing company. If your team doesn't, the gym or club can face a penalty. Policies differ by event, so your gym's travel coordinator is the final word.

How to book a team hotel without stress

  1. Get the housing link from your gym, not from Google. Copycat housing sites exist. Only use the link your coach or the event page gives you.
  2. Book the day the block opens. The closest hotels go first.
  3. Screenshot the cancellation policy. Housing-company reservations often have stricter deadlines than booking directly.
  4. Add your loyalty number. Call or message the hotel a few days before arrival and ask them to attach your Marriott Bonvoy number (or other program). Many block rates are still eligible to earn points, but confirm with the hotel.
  5. Ask for the rooming list deadline. Name changes and roommate swaps are usually locked after a certain date.

Can you use points on a stay-to-play hotel?

Sometimes. Award nights are booked directly with the hotel program, which may not count toward the event's block. Before redeeming points, ask your gym whether an award booking at a block hotel satisfies the housing policy. When it doesn't, I pay for the block and save points for non-housing events, family vacations, or the extra night before.

Quick answers

What is a stay-to-play hotel policy?

A rule used by many youth sports and cheer events where participating teams must book hotel rooms through the event's official housing block. The event earns hotel contract credit, and teams that book outside the block may face penalties.

Do you earn Marriott Bonvoy points on a team hotel block?

Often yes, if the rate is eligible and your member number is on the reservation. Ask the front desk to add it before check-in and confirm the rate qualifies.

Can I book a stay-to-play hotel with points?

Sometimes. Award bookings are made directly with the hotel program and may not count toward the event block. Ask your gym or club whether it satisfies the housing policy before you book.
